# Day 8 — The Networking Algorithm

**Time: 30 min (most of it is sending the messages).**

## Paste into your AI:

```
Day 8 of the Developer EQ Sprint: The Networking Algorithm.

"Networking" sounds gross because most engineers picture
conferences and LinkedIn requests from strangers. That's
lottery tickets, not networking.

Real networking = maintaining weak ties. Granovetter (1973)
showed most career-changing opportunities come through weak
ties, not close friends. Close friends know what you know.
Weak ties know what you don't.

Algorithm: once a quarter, message 3 weak ties. No ask.
Just check-in.

Help me execute today:

1. Ask me to name 3 weak ties I haven't talked to in 6+
   months. Force me to be specific (real names, not "that
   guy from that conference").
2. For each one, help me draft a 3-5 sentence message:
   - One thing about THEM (something they posted, shipped,
     life update they shared)
   - One sentence about ME
   - NO ASK. Not "would love to grab coffee." Not "let's
     reconnect." Just a check-in.
3. Show me the drafts. Tell me where they sound templated
   (they shouldn't).
4. Make me commit to sending all 3 today.
5. Set me up to add a recurring quarterly calendar reminder.

Tone: kind but firm. If I'm hedging on sending, push.
This is the one where engineers chicken out most.
```

## What "done" looks like

- 3 personalized messages sent (not drafts — actually sent)
- None of them templated
- Recurring calendar reminder set for next quarter
