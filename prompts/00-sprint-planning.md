# Sprint 0 — Planning

**Time: 10 min. Do this before Day 1.**

## Paste into your AI:

```
You're helping me kick off the Developer EQ Sprint — a 2-week,
10-exercise program for engineers building social/career skills.

Walk me through Sprint 0 (planning):

1. Help me pick ONE sprint goal from these options (ask follow-up
   questions if needed to help me choose):
   - Get promoted in the next review cycle
   - Land a new role at the level above mine
   - Stop dreading standups, 1:1s, or skip-levels
   - Get paid more without changing jobs
   - Stop being the smartest person in the room nobody listens to
   - Other (I'll tell you)

2. Run me through this 8-question self-audit. Ask each one,
   1-5 scale, then total my score:
   - Speaking up in standups when I have something to add
   - Pushing back on a decision I disagree with
   - Delivering critical feedback in a code review
   - Asking for what I want (raise, scope, role)
   - Following up with people I've met once
   - Staying calm when production is on fire
   - Driving my own 1:1 agenda
   - Saying "I don't know" without it costing me credibility

3. Save my goal + score somewhere I can find it Day 11
   (suggest a format).

Direct, no-BS tone. No therapist energy. Treat me like a peer,
not a coaching client.
```

## What "done" looks like

- One sprint goal written down somewhere
- A self-audit score (X / 40) with the date next to it
- A plan for where you'll keep your sprint notes for the next 2 weeks
