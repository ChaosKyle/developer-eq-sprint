# Day 9 — The Promotion Ask

**Time: 30-45 min. The most leveraged exercise in the sprint.**

## Paste into your AI:

```
Day 9 of the Developer EQ Sprint: The Promotion Ask.

I'm not getting promoted because I "deserve" it. Lots of
people deserve it. I'll get promoted because someone in a
calibration meeting can articulate, in one paragraph, why
I'm already operating at the next level.

That someone is supposed to be my manager. But my manager
has 8 reports, three competing initiatives, and forgets
half the stuff I did 6 months ago.

So I write the paragraph for them.

Help me draft my Promotion Brief. One page. Three sections:

1. WHAT LEVEL am I asking for, and why am I already
   operating there? (3 sentences max)
2. THREE SPECIFIC PROJECTS from the last 6 months that
   demonstrate it (1 paragraph each, with metrics)
3. ONE THING I'm working on that closes any remaining gap
   (1 sentence)

For section 2 — your job is to PULL THE METRICS OUT OF ME.
I will undersell every project. Ask probing questions:
- "How much time did that save? Per week? Annualized?"
- "How many people use this now?"
- "What was the revenue/cost impact?"
- "Did anyone get unblocked because of this?"
- "What was the alternative if you hadn't done it?"
- "Who would describe this as critical to the team?"

Don't accept "it just helped" or "people seemed to like it."
Numbers > adjectives. Always.

Then:
- Help me decide what to do with it: send to manager before
  next 1:1 / save for review cycle / personal brag doc
- If I'm sending it, draft the message that goes with it
  (frame it as "here's a brief I drafted on what I'm working
  toward — would love to talk through it next 1:1")

Tone: skeptical interviewer. If I claim I "led" something,
ask who actually decided. If I claim I "saved time," ask
how much, measured how. Make me earn every sentence.
```

## What "done" looks like

- One page, three sections, all complete
- Each project has at least one quantified outcome
- You've decided: send / save / brag-doc — and you know why
