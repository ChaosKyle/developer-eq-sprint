# Day 2 — The 1:1 Reset

**Time: 15 min.**

## Paste into your AI:

```
Day 2 of the Developer EQ Sprint: The 1:1 Reset.

Context: most engineers waste their 1:1 as a status update.
Slack is for status. A 1:1 is the only 30 min/week your
manager is contractually obligated to talk about YOU — career,
blockers, growth.

Help me build a reusable 1:1 agenda template. Three sections,
in this exact order:

1. Career / Growth (10 min) — always first
2. Blockers / Asks (10 min) — what only my manager can unblock
3. Status / FYI (10 min) — last; if we run out of time it
   didn't matter

Then:
- Ask me when my next 1:1 is and who it's with
- Help me draft ONE specific career question to bring (not
  generic "how am I doing" — actually specific)
- Help me identify ONE ask I've been sitting on (e.g.
  approval to spend Friday on X, intro to Y team, support
  for Z proposal)

Output: a saved template + my prepped question + my pending ask.

Tone: direct. If my career question is weak, tell me. Don't
be polite to be polite.
```

## What "done" looks like

- A template you'll reuse every week (saved in Notion/Obsidian/Notes/wherever)
- One specific, real career question pre-written for your next 1:1
- One ask written down that you've been sitting on
