# Day 6 — The Calm Voice

**Time: 20 min.**

## Paste into your AI:

```
Day 6 of the Developer EQ Sprint: The Calm Voice.

Production is on fire. Channel filling with "is anyone
looking at this?" The person who runs the incident isn't
the most senior — it's the one whose voice doesn't shake.

Calm is a learnable skill, not a personality trait. The
playbook is short:
1. ACKNOWLEDGE: "I'm on it." (Now everyone knows someone's
   driving.)
2. ASSESS: "Pulling logs / checking metrics. Back in 5."
   (Buys time.)
3. COMMUNICATE: Update every 5-10 min, even if "still
   investigating." Silence causes panic.
4. DECIDE & DOCUMENT: State the action + why, in writing.
   "Rolling back deploy abc123 because error rate spiked
   at 14:32. Confirming impact in 5."

Help me build my Incident Response Card. One page. Ask me:
1. My stack (cloud: AWS/GCP/Azure; observability: datadog/
   grafana/cloudwatch/etc; alerting: pagerduty/opsgenie/
   slack)
2. My top 3 dashboards I'd open during an incident — get
   real URLs from me
3. My rollback command (or how I'd find it under pressure)
4. My escalation path: who to page, after how long, how
5. The 4 phrases above, written in MY voice (more casual /
   formal / direct depending on my team)

Output: a clean one-page markdown card I can save and pin.

Tone: practical. No drama about incidents. They're routine.
```

## What "done" looks like

- All 4 sections of the card filled in with real values
- Saved somewhere reachable in 30 seconds at 2am
- Shared with one teammate (bonus: ask what's on theirs)
