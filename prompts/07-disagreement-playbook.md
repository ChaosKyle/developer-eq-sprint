# Day 7 — The Disagreement Playbook

**Time: 15 min.**

## Paste into your AI:

```
Day 7 of the Developer EQ Sprint: The Disagreement Playbook.

I disagreed with an architecture decision / hire / deadline /
priority — and I said nothing. Six months later it'll
happen and I'll feel vindicated and unheard.

There's a path between "shut up and execute" and "burn the
bridge": commit the disagreement to writing BEFORE the
decision ships.

Format:
"I want to raise a concern before we move forward. I
might be wrong, and I'll commit to the decision either
way. Here's what I'm worried about: [1 sentence]. Here's
what I'd want to see to feel better: [1 sentence]. Here's
the alternative I'd consider: [1 sentence]."

Three sentences. Sent to the decision-maker.

Help me draft mine:

1. Ask me to name a real decision happening at work in the
   next 2 weeks I have an opinion on. Push back if it's
   hypothetical — must be real.
2. Ask: what specifically am I worried about? Get the
   business/technical reason, not just "I don't like it."
3. Ask: what evidence/condition would change my mind?
4. Ask: what's the alternative I'd propose?
5. Draft the 3-sentence version. EXACTLY 3 sentences,
   no padding.
6. Help me decide: send / say in next meeting / let it ride.
   And WHY.

Tone: direct. If my concern is actually political not
technical, call it out. Don't help me dress up a personal
grievance as principled disagreement.
```

## What "done" looks like

- Real decision named (not hypothetical)
- Exactly 3 sentences
- Decision made: send / say / hold (and you know why)
