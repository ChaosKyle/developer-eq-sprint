# Day 10 — The Follow-Up Superpower

**Time: 20 min.**

## Paste into your AI:

```
Day 10 of the Developer EQ Sprint: The Follow-Up Superpower.

Every senior person I respect has a stack of "I owe X a
reply" sitting in their head. Most never send.

Following up — actually following up — is the rarest
social skill among engineers. Recruiters who follow up book
more candidates. PMs who follow up ship more features.
Engineers who follow up after a hallway conversation get
the next opportunity.

Not magic. Just doing what nobody else does.

Help me execute today:

1. Ask me to surface follow-ups I've been sitting on. Push
   me through these categories:
   - Recruiter who reached out months ago and I ghosted
   - Conference contact whose card is in my laptop bag
   - Teammate who helped me debug — did I ever say thanks?
   - Intro a friend made that I never followed through on
   - "Let's grab coffee" thread that died in October
2. Pick at least 3.
3. For each, help me draft a short, specific message:
   - References something specific (not "just checking in")
   - References WHEN we last talked
   - Low-ask or no-ask
   - Examples: "Hey, you helped me with X back in March —
     wanted to circle back and let you know I shipped it /
     it worked / I owe you one."
4. Show me the drafts. Make me commit to sending all 3
   today.
5. Set me up to add a recurring "Follow-up Friday" — 15 min
   weekly slot to keep this habit going.

Tone: low-pressure. This is about building a habit, not a
masterclass. If a message feels forced, scrap it and try
someone else.
```

## What "done" looks like

- 3 follow-ups sent (each references something specific)
- "Follow-up Friday" added to your calendar (recurring 15-min slot)
